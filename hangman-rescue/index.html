Hangman Rescue: Word Quest

A friendly hangman word game in 10 languages.

Guess the word letter by letter and rescue Kiki's balloon. World Tour with 8 worlds and 120 levels, a Daily Word, Quick Play and a two-player mode. Available in English, Türkçe, Español, Português, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski and Bahasa Indonesia.
